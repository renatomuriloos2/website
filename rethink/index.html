The chemistry

Four lines. Every fiber.

Everything we make exists to do more with less inside a dye house. Every product is concentrated, so when they run together there is no need for anti-foam.

THINK²DYE

Dye, without the waste.

A complete dyeing line for natural, artificial, and synthetic fibers: concentrated auxiliaries, specialized biotech, and reactive dyes built to cut resource use.

Soft, no matter the fiber.

Softeners for every fiber and fabric, applied by foulard, exhaustion, or spray, built to prevent foulard stop marks, seam holes, and precipitate stains.

Why we exist

Together we can make a better world for a future generation.

Textile production is one of the world's largest users of water. We exist to change what that costs: processes and chemicals that preserve natural resources and improve life for the people who depend on this industry.

To transform the world, we need to be surrounded by the right people. With an open mind and an innovative attitude, we can break every paradigm the industry has settled for.

The questions mills actually ask.

Will it work with our fibers? +
Yes. THINK²DYE covers natural, artificial, and synthetic fibers, and the lab confirms fit on your fabric before anything ships.
Do we need new machines? +
No. Our chemistry is made for standard dyeing and finishing equipment. A pilot starts with your current process, not a rebuild.
How do we know the sustainability claims are real? +
You check them. ZDHC MRSL V2.0 Level 3, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, OEKO-TEX, and California Prop 65 compliance are third-party verified, and we share the documentation during your pilot.
Where are you? +
Our plant and laboratory are in Zona Libre ZipTex, Choloma, Cortés, Honduras, in the heart of Central America's textile corridor.
